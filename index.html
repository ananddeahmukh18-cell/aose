Step 1: Camera Setup

Important Instructions:

  1. Stand 6 feet (2 meters) away from the camera
  2. Wear form-fitting clothes (t-shirt/tank top)
  3. Stand against a plain background
  4. Keep your arms slightly away from your body
  5. Make sure you're in good lighting

Step 2: Take Photo

Hold a standard object for scale reference:

Please hold a credit card, ID card, or smartphone against your shoulder area. This helps us calculate accurate measurements.
